While you wait: make the covers

The covers are free to make and print at home. Load your StoryGraph or Goodreads library, or search for any book, and print the sheet at the same size as your books.

Make your covers

When they arrive

  1. Print the covers at 100% ("Actual size") on matte sticker paper and check the 50 mm ruler.
  2. Cut each cover out and wrap it from the back, over the spine, to the front, pressing it down.
  3. Hang them up, or stand them on a shelf.

More ideas are in the mini book ornaments guide and the year in books ornament guide.
