Questions

Which size for Christmas ornaments?

For a full-size Christmas tree, 1:4 (52.5 x 35 mm, about 2 inches tall), or 1:5 (42 x 28 mm) for a smaller look. 1:7 (30 x 20 mm) suits a small tabletop tree. 1:12 suits dollhouses and 1:6 fashion dolls.

Can I return them?

Every book is made to order, so orders can't be returned. If yours arrives damaged or never arrives, email support@minibookornaments.com for a reprint or a refund.
