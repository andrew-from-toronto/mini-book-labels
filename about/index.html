About Mini Book Ornaments

Hi, I'm Andrew. I make mini book ornaments in Mississauga, Ontario: the free cover designer on this site, and blank books printed to order for anyone without a 3D printer.

The books

Each blank is printed to order on my own printers in PLA, at the size and with the options you choose, then checked and packed by hand. Mini Book Ornaments is a registered business in Ontario, Canada.

Ordering

  • Prices are in Canadian dollars. No sales tax is added.
  • Payment is by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe directly; they never reach this site.
  • You get an email as soon as your order is placed, and another with tracking when it ships.

Shipping

  • Orders ship within a week, with tracking.
  • Shipping to Canada, the US, the UK, Australia and New Zealand, priced at checkout for your address.
  • US orders ship with duties paid, so there's nothing more to pay on delivery.

Returns

Every book is made to order, so orders can't be returned. If yours arrives damaged or never arrives, email me and I'll reprint it or refund you.

Privacy

  • No ads, no tracking cookies. Visits are counted anonymously.
  • If you arrive from a campaign link, its name is kept in your browser for 30 days and saved with any order you place.
  • Your library and covers stay in your browser; they're never uploaded.
  • Your name, email and address are used only to make, ship and support your order.

Contact

Questions about an order, a custom size or anything else: support@minibookornaments.com
