Mini book ornaments from the books you love

Mini Book Labels makes printable wraparound covers (back, spine and front) for miniature books and mini book ornaments. Load your StoryGraph or Goodreads library, or search for any book, pick a scale from 1:4 to 1:24 (1:12 for dollhouses), and print a sheet of covers at actual size.

It also builds a free 3D printable hardcover book, sized to your covers, with an optional ornament hook, and shows the finished book in 3D before you print.
